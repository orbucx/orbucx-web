The Cloud Audit

Before we fix anything,

Every engagement starts with a comprehensive, free audit of your cloud. Senior engineers review the full stack and hand you a findings report with prioritized fixes — yours to keep, whoever executes it.

Infrastructure & architecture

How your environments are built, provisioned, and kept in sync — and where drift, single points of failure, and manual snowflakes hide.

Reliability & recovery

A written findings report with every issue ranked by impact and effort, quantified savings and risks, and a prioritized 90-day plan. First findings within 24 hours; full report within 5 business days.

FAQ

Questions clients actually ask

What does the free cloud audit include?

A full review of your cloud across five dimensions: infrastructure and architecture, CI/CD pipelines, security posture, costs, and reliability. You grant read-only access (we'll send exact IAM policies), first findings arrive within 24 hours, and within 5 business days you get a written report with every issue prioritized by impact and effort. No obligation to continue — the report is yours either way.

What access do you need, and how do you handle security?

We start with least-privilege, read-only roles and only escalate per approved scope. All access goes through your IAM with full audit trails — no shared credentials, ever. We're happy to sign your NDA and security agreements before seeing anything.

Can you build our cloud from scratch?

Yes. Greenfield is some of our favorite work: new AWS, GCP, or Azure environments designed right from day one — infrastructure as code, CI/CD, monitoring, and security baked in from the first commit instead of retrofitted later. We also handle migrations from on-prem or legacy accounts.

How do retainers work?

A retainer gives you a dedicated senior engineer with guaranteed monthly hours covering whatever your cloud needs: maintenance, monitoring, incident response, security reviews, cost control, and new build work. You get documented runbooks, a monthly review, and a direct Slack line. Cancel with 30 days' notice — no annual lock-in.

Which clouds and tools do you cover?

AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure as first-class citizens, plus on-prem-to-cloud migrations. Standard toolkit: Terraform, Pulumi, Kubernetes, Docker, GitHub Actions/GitLab CI, Prometheus/Grafana, Datadog. If you run something else, ask — the network is broad.

What's the minimum engagement?

The free audit is the smallest way to start — no commitment at all. Paid engagements begin with a fixed-price starter engagement (from $999) for a single focused outcome; projects start around $6,000; retainers from $3,900/month with a 30-day notice period.

You're a boutique firm — what if you're unavailable?

Every retainer includes documented runbooks and a vetted backup engineer briefed on your stack. Because everything we build is code-managed and documented, any competent engineer — ours or yours — can operate it.
