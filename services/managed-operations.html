Managed Operations FAQ

What's the difference between retainer and project work?

Retainers are ongoing — monitoring, maintenance, support, and a bucket of hours for new work each month. Projects are one-time with defined scope — migrations, new setups, big initiatives. Many clients do both.

Do unused hours roll over?

By default, no. But we're flexible — if you're consistently under, we'll adjust your plan. If you have a big month, we can flex up. The goal is you get value, not that we squeeze every hour.

What if we need 24/7 coverage?

Available on Growth and Enterprise tiers. We have engineers across time zones for true 24/7 coverage. Cost depends on response time SLAs and complexity.

Can we scale up temporarily?

Yes. Launching a big feature? Expecting traffic spike? We can add hours for specific months. Just give us heads up so we can allocate capacity.

What if we want to bring DevOps in-house later?

Great! We'll help you hire and transition. Everything we build is documented and designed to be handed over. No vendor lock-in, no proprietary tools.
