FinOps FAQ

How quickly will we see savings?

Quick wins (zombie cleanup, obvious right-sizing) show up in the next billing cycle. Savings plans take effect immediately after purchase. Full optimization typically 4-8 weeks.

Will right-sizing affect performance?

No. We analyze actual utilization over weeks/months, not just a snapshot. We recommend changes that maintain headroom for peaks. Every change is tested and reversible.

Should we use savings plans or reserved instances?

Savings Plans (AWS) are usually better — more flexible, apply across instance types/regions. Reserved Instances only for specific, unchanging workloads. We model your usage to recommend the optimal mix.

Are spot instances risky?

For the right workloads, no. Stateless services, batch jobs, dev/test environments are ideal. We implement proper interruption handling and mixed instance strategies. Never for databases or stateful services.

Do you work on commission?

No. We charge fixed project fees or hourly rates. No percentage of savings, no conflict of interest. Our goal is to optimize, not maximize our cut.
