Cloud Migration FAQ

How long does a typical migration take?

Simple lift-and-shift: 2-4 weeks. Replatforming with containerization: 4-12 weeks. Large enterprise migrations: 3-12 months. We scope precisely based on your inventory and complexity.

Can you really migrate with zero downtime?

For most applications, yes. We use blue-green deployments, database replication, and traffic shifting. Some components (like databases during final sync) may need brief read-only windows. We design to minimize and communicate any required windows.

Lift-and-shift or replatform?

Depends on your goals. Lift-and-shift is faster and lower risk — good when you need to exit a datacenter quickly. Replatforming captures more cloud benefits but takes longer. We often recommend: lift first, optimize later.

What about compliance during migration?

We maintain compliance throughout. Data encryption in transit and at rest, audit trails for all changes, proper IAM controls. For regulated industries, we ensure the cloud architecture meets requirements before migration begins.
