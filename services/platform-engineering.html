Platform Engineering FAQ

When should we invest in platform engineering?

When developer friction is slowing down the business. Signs: long lead times for new services, DevOps team is a bottleneck, inconsistent practices across teams, slow onboarding. Usually makes sense at 20+ engineers.

Backstage or something else?

Backstage (Spotify) is the open-source leader — highly customizable, strong community. Commercial options (Port, Cortex, OpsLevel) are easier to start with. We recommend based on your team size and customization needs.

How do you balance self-service with security?

Policy as code. Resources are provisioned through templates that enforce security standards. Developers get freedom within guardrails — they can't accidentally create public S3 buckets or over-permissive IAM roles.

Do we need a dedicated platform team?

Eventually, yes. You can start with DevOps/SRE folks spending part-time on platform work. At ~50 engineers, a dedicated platform team (2-4 people) usually pays off. We can help you structure this.
