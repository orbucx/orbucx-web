UK DevOps FAQ

Do you have an office in London?

We're remote-first but London-available. We can meet in person for kickoffs, strategy sessions, or when it matters. For day-to-day work, Slack and video calls are more efficient.

What about UK data residency?

We design infrastructure with UK data residency by default. AWS eu-west-2 (London), GCP europe-west2 (London), Azure UK South. Your data stays in the UK.

Do you understand FCA requirements?

Yes. We've helped multiple UK fintechs achieve and maintain FCA-compliant infrastructure. Operational resilience, third-party risk management, and incident reporting built in.

What's the pricing in GBP?

We quote in USD for consistency, but we're happy to invoice in GBP. Retainers start at approximately £3,100/month (at current rates).

Can you work with our existing UK MSP?

Absolutely. We often work alongside existing IT providers. We focus on DevOps and cloud, they handle end-user support and networking. Clear boundaries, no conflict.
